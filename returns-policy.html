Holiday Returns & Exchanges

Shop with absolute peace of mind. Every holiday purchase is backed by our extended 60-day return guarantee until January 31, 2025.

🛡️

Extended Holiday Return Window — Active Until Jan 31, 2025

We want your loved ones to adore every treasure. All items purchased between October 1 and December 24, 2024 can be returned or exchanged through January 31, 2025 in original, unused condition with presentation gift packaging intact.

How Our Stress-Free Return Process Works

Complete your return or gift exchange in four seamless steps from your home:

1

Start Online

Enter your Order Number (#NOEL-XXXXX) or Gift Receipt code to initiate your request.

2

Get Prepaid Label

Instantly download and print your prepaid FedEx (USA) or DHL Express (EU) return shipping label.

3

Pack & Drop Off

Place items in their protective box, attach the label, and hand it to any authorized carrier drop-off point.

4

Prompt Refund

Once inspected, refunds are credited back to your original payment method in 3–5 business days.

🎁 Discreet Holiday Gift Exchanges

Received a NOËL & CO. gift and would prefer a different fragrance or collection item? We offer anonymous gift exchanges.

You can exchange your gift for an alternate item or store credit voucher without notifying the generous gift-giver. Simply contact our Holiday Concierge at support@noelchristmas.com with the order number listed on your packing slip.

✨ Gift Exchange Bonus: Gift exchanges and store credit returns are 100% free with no restocking fees or return shipping deductions applied.

📦 Return Condition Guidelines

  • Artisanal Candles: Must be unlit with the wick intact, in their original embossed gift box with brass lids.
  • Mouth-Blown Glass Baubles: Must be in pristine, unhung condition in the satin-lined velvet presentation box.
  • Merino Wool Blankets & Textiles: Must be unused, unwashed, with the original ribbon ties and authenticity tag intact.
  • Handcrafted Confections & Chocolates: Due to food safety standards, edible holiday treats cannot be returned once opened unless damaged in transit.

🛡️ Fragile Item Breakage Protection

We take extraordinary care in packing our mouth-blown glass ornaments and ceramic jars with custom-molded biodegradable shock absorbers. However, in the rare event of damage during transatlantic transit:

  • Take a photo of the damaged item and packaging within 7 days of delivery.
  • Email it to support@noelchristmas.com.
  • We will dispatch a complimentary expedited replacement within 24 hours with zero paperwork required.

💳 Refund Processing Timelines

Once our fulfillment center in Ohio (USA) or Frankfurt (Germany) receives and inspects your return, we will process your refund immediately:

  • Shop Pay, Apple Pay & Credit Cards: 3 to 5 business days depending on your bank.
  • PayPal: Instant to 24 hours.
  • Store Credit & Gift Cards: Issued instantly via email upon package scan.
