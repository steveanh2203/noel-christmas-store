How Can We Help You?

Clear answers about our guaranteed Christmas delivery, luxury gift packaging, extended returns, and artisan creations.

🚚 Shipping, Couriers & Holiday Deadlines

Yes, 100% guaranteed! As long as you place your order before our holiday cutoff dates (December 18 for US Standard / EU Standard, or December 20 for FedEx Priority & DHL Express), your package is guaranteed to arrive at your door on or before December 24. If your order arrives late, we will refund your purchase 100% and you keep the items for free.

We operate dedicated transatlantic fulfillment centers in Columbus, Ohio (USA) and Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Domestic US orders ship via FedEx Priority and USPS Express. European orders ship via DHL Express with full end-to-end GPS satellite tracking.

As soon as your holiday treasures are packaged, you will receive an automated email and SMS notification containing your direct tracking link. You can also track your order at any moment on our Live Order Tracking page using your order number (#NOEL-XXXXX).

🔄 Extended Returns & Breakage Protection

All purchases placed between October 1 and December 24, 2024 are covered by our Extended Holiday Return window until January 31, 2025. Items must be in unlit/unhung original condition with presentation gift packaging. See our Full Return Policy for complete details.

We provide a 100% Fragile Breakage Guarantee. If anything arrives cracked or broken, simply snap a photo and email support@noelchristmas.com. We will dispatch a complimentary express replacement within 24 hours with zero hassle or claim forms.

🕯️ Materials & Candle Care

Our candles are hand-poured with a 100% pure organic coconut-soy wax blend infused with essential oils and phthalate-free European fine fragrances. We use lead-free organic cotton wicks paired with crackling natural hardwood dual wicks for a soothing fireplace crackle and zero black soot.

On your first burn, allow the candle to burn for 3 to 4 hours until the melted wax pool reaches all edges of the glass jar. Always trim the wick to 1/4 inch (6mm) before lighting to ensure a steady, fragrant 65-hour total burn time.
