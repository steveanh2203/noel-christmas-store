The Art of the Festive Season

Preserving the magic of traditional Christmas craftsmanship for homes across North America and Europe.

Born from a Love of European Winter Traditions

Founded in 2018, NOËL & CO. was born out of a desire to bring genuine warmth, nostalgia, and authentic artisan crafts back to the Christmas holiday. In a world saturated with disposable plastic decor, we curate heirloom treasures made by generational artisans in Germany, Poland, France, and North America.

Every mouth-blown glass bauble is shaped over a flame by master glassmakers; every candle is hand-poured with natural botanical wax; and every wool throw is woven from ethical Merino wool.

Our Three Guiding Commitments

🌿
100% Eco-Conscious Packaging Every box, tape strip, and ribbon we use is biodegradable or FSC-certified recyclable.
⭐
Fair Trade Artisan Partnerships We support small multi-generational European and American workshops, ensuring living wages and craft preservation.
✈️
Seamless Transatlantic Fulfillment Dual fulfillment warehouses in Columbus, Ohio and Frankfurt, Germany guarantee lightning-fast regional delivery.
